# AnjinMeili: monthly activity digest

What moved in [AnjinMeili](https://github.com/AnjinMeili)'s LED-related repositories, month by month. Anjin (James Hutchinson) contributes to MoonLight with an agent team he calls Friday, and this page covers the rest of his GitHub: his own repositories about LED panels, light shows and music-reactive visuals, and his forks that carry commits of his own. Forks he left untouched, his MoonLight fork (its work arrives here as pull requests) and his laser fabrication repositories are left out. External-context reference, a factual log of a friend's activity, not MoonLight's own history or roadmap. Newest month on top. The reusable prompt that generates these lives in [index.md](index.md).

**Branch note, the fork work lives in named branches and upstream pull requests.** His forks' default branches track their parents; the commits of his own sit in branches named for the change (`rp1-pio-drain`, `moonlight`, `review-2026-11-12`, `pixelpusher`) and go upstream as pull requests, so each month reads those branches and the pull requests, not the default branch. MatrixPrism's public repository is an export of a private development repository: each commit is a squashed snapshot, so the month is read from what each export changed.

## The repositories

- [matrix-marquee](https://github.com/AnjinMeili/matrix-marquee), his own, Python (with JavaScript for the page), `main`. MatrixMarquee: offline-first signage for a Raspberry Pi driving an RGB LED matrix of any size (the first display is 96x64), controlled from a phone over the sign's own Wi-Fi. Messages in display fonts with color styles, motion, reveals and animated borders, pictures and videos with words on top, playlists with entrance effects, live brightness and gamma, and the Pi's heat and power on the page; the panel is driven through rpi-rgb-led-matrix's Python bindings.
- [matrixprism](https://github.com/AnjinMeili/matrixprism), his own, JavaScript (the composer and the desktop app) and C++ (the receiver), `main`, GPL-3.0. MatrixPrism: a real-time composer of music-driven generative visuals for LED panels, 148 patterns in a quad mixer, streamed as DDP to WLED, to any DDP receiver, or to its own `matrix-dmx`, a Raspberry Pi receiver for HUB75 panels that takes Art-Net, sACN and DDP, answers as a WLED node, and records and plays back what it shows. Pre-releases v0.1.0 and v0.1.1 on 2026-10-27.
- [rpi-rgb-led-matrix](https://github.com/AnjinMeili/rpi-rgb-led-matrix), a fork of [hzeller/rpi-rgb-led-matrix](https://github.com/hzeller/rpi-rgb-led-matrix), C++, `master` tracking upstream. The Raspberry Pi HUB75 library; his work is on the Pi 5 (RP1 PIO) backend, in the branches `rp1-pio-drain` and `piolib-bsd-3-clause`, each an upstream pull request.
- [rpi-utils](https://github.com/AnjinMeili/rpi-utils), a fork of [raspberrypi/utils](https://github.com/raspberrypi/utils), C, `master` tracking upstream. Raspberry Pi's own utilities, among them `piolib`, the library for the Pi 5's PIO; his work is one commit on the branch `moonlight`.
- [WLED-DJ](https://github.com/AnjinMeili/WLED-DJ), a fork of [haslanli/WLED-DJ](https://github.com/haslanli/WLED-DJ), Python and Swift, `main` tracking upstream. A macOS framework for music-reactive WLED lights (system-audio capture, analysis every 10 ms, live stem separation, sync by reported latency, DDP out) that ships no effects of its own; his work is a review of it on the branch `review-2026-11-12`.
- [ILDAWaveX16V2](https://github.com/AnjinMeili/ILDAWaveX16V2), a fork of [stanleyondrus/ILDAWaveX16V2](https://github.com/stanleyondrus/ILDAWaveX16V2), C, `main` equal to upstream. A dual-processor laser DAC (ESP32-S3 for network, web page, SD playback and protocols; RP2354 for the real-time output) with ILDA output and Ether Dream and IDN streaming; his five pull requests of October 2026 (SD browsing and ILDA preview, scanner protection, color shift, streaming fixes) are merged upstream.
- [WLED-MM](https://github.com/AnjinMeili/WLED-MM), a fork of [MoonModules/WLED-MM](https://github.com/MoonModules/WLED-MM), C++, `mdev` tracking upstream. His branch `pixelpusher` (March 2026) adds a PixelPusher protocol usermod, enabled in the default builds, with no pull request upstream.
- [fibonacci128-touch-aurora](https://github.com/AnjinMeili/fibonacci128-touch-aurora), his own, C, default branch `aurora`. Aurora patterns added to Jason Coon's Fibonacci128 touch demo (FastLED on a SAMD21), with a Phyllotaxis Bloom pattern and a Python simulator that renders it; the pattern went upstream as [fibonacci128-touch-demo #3](https://github.com/jasoncoon/fibonacci128-touch-demo/pull/3), merged 2026-05-11. Quiet since 2026-03-30.
- [AuroraDrop-LXP](https://github.com/AnjinMeili/AuroraDrop-LXP), a fork of [troyhacks/AuroraDrop-LXP](https://github.com/troyhacks/AuroraDrop-LXP), C++, `main`. An audio visualizer for an ESP32 with a HUB75 matrix and an INMP441 microphone; two fixes of his from July 2024, quiet since.
- Two dormant repositories of his own: [SmartMatrix-Aurora-IR-Web-Gateway](https://github.com/AnjinMeili/SmartMatrix-Aurora-IR-Web-Gateway) (2019, an ESP8266 web page that sends a SmartMatrix remote's IR codes) and [1616-RGB-LED-Matrix](https://github.com/AnjinMeili/1616-RGB-LED-Matrix) (2016, Processing sketches for a 16x16 matrix).

## November 2026

**matrix-marquee**, 2 commits:

- Renamed from Protest to MatrixMarquee and released as 0.1.0, with the one-tap "Protest" look now "Impact"; installed systems keep their `protest` service name and paths.
- Live updates: every open page sees who else is connected (each phone can set its name), picks up changes made from another phone, and is told at once about heat or power problems and skipped items (a new events module, 224 lines, and its tests).
- An operator PIN: an access module (222 lines) with operator routes, so changing the sign needs a PIN while viewing does not.
- The sign's own Wi-Fi name becomes `<hostname>.local` or `<hostname>.home.arpa`, chosen in `ap.env` (`.home` is left out, citing RFC 8375).
- A SECURITY.md with the security policy.

**matrixprism**, 1 export (2026-11-04, 31 files, +2,879):

- NDI output in a separate NDI build of the desktop app, never in a release: the composed picture, lifted by the video gamma and enlarged by a whole multiple, as an NDI source through the NDI runtime installed from ndi.video, called through koffi; a stand-in runtime compiled from NDI's headers checks it, a real NDI receiver has not seen it yet.
- Two HUB75 refresh-rate papers in `docs/`: the refresh a chain reaches by chain length and bits per channel, worked out from shift and bit-plane timings, with tables for 8, 11 and 16 bits and the same arithmetic for ColorLight cards; one of the two is written in MoonLight's documentation style.
- A tempo plan: evolving troyhacks' tempo tracker (the comb, the log-normal prior and the phase-locked loop from his WLED audio-reactive usermod) one measured change at a time for the composer, ESP32 and Teensy, scored within 2% on 774 Beatport previews and 296 Freesound loops, where the faithful port reads 69.5% and MatrixPrism's offline fold 90.6%.
- A `docs/checks.md` naming every check the project runs, and the roadmap's next steps updated. No release in November.

**rpi-rgb-led-matrix**, 3 commits on two branches, 2 pull requests and 2 issues upstream:

- `rp1-pio-drain` (2 commits, 2026-11-04, upstream [#1910](https://github.com/hzeller/rpi-rgb-led-matrix/pull/1910), open): the Pi 5 PIO backend let in-flight DMA drain before disabling the state machine, since disabling it with queued words left the next PIO session blocked forever; then the teardown in the standard order (wait, blank, disable, clear the FIFOs, release the pins, remove the program, unclaim, close), waiting as long as three frames need, since a fixed 0.5 s left a 64x2048 chain at 11 bits hanging.
- `piolib-bsd-3-clause` (1 commit, 2026-11-15, upstream [#1915](https://github.com/hzeller/rpi-rgb-led-matrix/pull/1915), merged the same day): nine vendored piolib files carried GPL-2.0 headers that Raspberry Pi had corrected to BSD-3-Clause in 2025; this applies the same change (his issue [#1914](https://github.com/hzeller/rpi-rgb-led-matrix/issues/1914)).
- Issue [#1911](https://github.com/hzeller/rpi-rgb-led-matrix/issues/1911) (open): measured shift times and refresh-rate tables by chain length and `--led-pwm-bits` on a Pi 5, the measurements behind MatrixPrism's refresh papers.
- Beside the fork, issue [Adafruit Piomatter #95](https://github.com/adafruit/Adafruit_Blinka_Raspberry_Pi5_Piomatter/issues/95) (2026-11-13, open) asks whether the protomatter PIO program, which rpi-rgb-led-matrix vendors for its Pi 5 backend, could be offered under a GPL-3.0-compatible license.

**rpi-utils**, 1 commit:

- `moonlight` (2026-11-18, on top of upstream's 2026-11-13 `rp1_ioctl` fix): piolib returns RP1 communication errors (`-EREMOTEIO`, `-ETIMEDOUT`) to the caller instead of ending the process through `pio_panic()`, so a program driving a panel can blank it when a state machine stops draining. No pull request upstream yet.

**WLED-DJ**, 2 commits:

- `review-2026-11-12`: a 318-line review of WLED-DJ at `9e05f5d` for its author, co-signed with Claude Opus 5.5 and saying how the work was split: what stands out, tempo and harmony-angle accuracy measured through WLED-DJ's own replay bench against the Beatport (774) and Freesound (296) sets beside the QM beat tracker, BpmDj, Beat This! and MatrixPrism's fold, eleven suggestions with file and line, and related projects. Documentation only.
